# Basket demand by hour

Basket size is measured as product lines in an order. This dataset does not provide item quantities or inventory/stockout records.

- Most orders: 10:00 (271,885 orders)
- Most product-order rows: 10:00 (2,764,426 product-order rows)
- Largest average basket: 22:00 (11.03 product lines per order)

Files: `product_lines_by_hour.csv`, `product_lines_by_hour.png`, and `average_basket_size_by_hour.png`.
