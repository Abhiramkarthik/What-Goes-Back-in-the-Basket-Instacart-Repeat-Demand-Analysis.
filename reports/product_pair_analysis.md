# Product pairs in the same basket

This analysis considers the 20 most frequently ordered products and counts baskets containing each pair. Lift compares the observed pair frequency with the frequency expected if the two products appeared independently.

- Most common pair: Bag of Organic Bananas + Organic Hass Avocado (62,341 orders; lift 2.47).

Lift above 1 means the pair occurs together more often than expected from their individual popularity. Pair counts and lift are descriptive associations, not causal recommendations.
Files: `top_product_pairs.csv`, `product_pairs_by_lift.csv`, and `top_product_pairs.png`.
