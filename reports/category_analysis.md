# Category demand and reorder patterns

Counts are product-order rows, not physical units. Reorder rate is weighted: reorder rows divided by all product-order rows in the category.

- Highest-volume department: produce — 9,479,291 product-order rows across 1,684 products (65.0% reorder rate).
- Highest-volume aisle: fresh fruits — 3,642,188 product-order rows across 382 products (71.8% reorder rate).
- Highest department reorder rate: dairy eggs (67.0%).
- Highest aisle reorder rate: milk (78.1% across 891,015 product-order rows).

Files: `department_demand_summary.csv`, `aisle_demand_summary.csv`, `department_demand_top10.png`, and `department_reorder_rate_top10.png`.

These are historical purchasing patterns. The dataset does not report stock availability or actual stockouts.
