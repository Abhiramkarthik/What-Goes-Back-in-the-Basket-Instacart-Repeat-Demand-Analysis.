# Customer order intervals

The first order per customer has no previous-order interval and is excluded.

- Most common interval band: 0–7 days (1,620,033 intervals; 50.4% of recorded intervals).

Files: `customer_return_intervals.csv` and `customer_return_intervals.png`.

This describes observed timing between orders in this historical sample. It is not a forecast of when an individual customer will order again.
